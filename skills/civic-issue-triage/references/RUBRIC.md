# Triage rubric

## Categories and responsible department
| category | typical department |
|---|---|
| pothole, road_damage | Road & Transport |
| traffic_sign | Traffic Police / Road & Transport |
| streetlight | Electrical Department |
| garbage | Solid Waste Management |
| water_leakage | Water Supply Department |
| waterlogging | Drainage & Sewerage |
| fallen_tree | Parks & Horticulture |
| other | General Administration |

Department names are generic. Adapt them to the user's city or municipality.

## Severity (0-100)
Judge from physical extent, danger to people or vehicles, and whether passage is blocked.

| score | band | meaning | example |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0-33 | Low | minor inconvenience | small crack, a little litter |
| 34-66 | Moderate | needs attention soon | overflowing bin, partial streetlight outage |
| 67-100 | Critical | hazard needing urgent action | deep pothole in a live lane, exposed wires, tree across the road |

## Community priority (used by the CivicLens platform)
priority = 40% severity + 30% community confirmations (capped at 25) + 15% age (capped at 14 days) + 15% category risk.

## Writing the complaint
2-3 formal, factual sentences. Describe only what is visible. Do not invent measurements, names or places.
